# SMKN 69 Jakarta Student Point System Framework

This document outlines the point deduction, point accumulation, and enforcement stages for student behavior management.

---

## 1. Cumulative Point Qualification
Student point accumulation remains active throughout their entire enrollment at SMKN 69 Jakarta[cite: 1].

* **0 – 25 Points:** Good[cite: 1]
* **26 – 74 Points:** Fair[cite: 1]
* **75 – 100 Points:** Poor[cite: 1]

---

## 2. Enforcement & Intervention Stages (Point Accumulation)
Points are added whenever a student commits a violation[cite: 1]. Interventions follow a tiered structure based on total cumulative points[cite: 1]:

* **Stage I (25 Points - Warning I / K-1):** 
* **Stage II (50 Points - Warning II / K-2):** 
* **Stage III (75 Points - Warning III / K-3):** 
* **Stage IV (100 Points - Final Case Handling):** 
---

## 3. Point Deductions (Reward Points)
Violation points are reduced when a student achieves academic/non-academic honors or actively participates in school programs[cite: 1]:

* **Active Role / Task Force** (OSIS/MPK, Waste Sorting, Composting, Urban Farming, Class/Extracurricular Officer, Committees): **-5 Points**[cite: 1]
* **School Level Achievement:** **-10 Points**[cite: 1]
* **Sub-district Level Achievement:** **-15 Points**[cite: 1]
* **District/City Level Achievement:** **-25 Points**[cite: 1]
* **Provincial Level Achievement:** **-50 Points**[cite: 1]
* **National Level Achievement:** **-75 Points**[cite: 1]
* **International Level Achievement:** **-100 Points**[cite: 1]

*(Note: Accumulating 100 reward points earns a student a learning support merchandise award from the school[cite: 1]).*

## 4. Violation Point Index

### A. Minor to Moderate Violations (5 – 10 Points)[cite: 1]
* **5 Points:** Incomplete uniform attributes[cite: 1], missing school cap/logo belt/prescribed shoes/socks[cite: 1], modified non-standard motorcycle exhaust[cite: 1], improper use of restrooms[cite: 1], untidy/fitted uniforms[cite: 1], altered/tapered trousers[cite: 1], eating/sleeping during class[cite: 1], causing minor class disruptions[cite: 1], late arrival to class/school[cite: 1], unfulfilled homework/piket duties[cite: 1], unkempt/dyed hair[cite: 1], wearing makeup (lipstick, eyebrow pencil, powder, etc.) for female students[cite: 1], wearing prohibited accessories for male students[cite: 1].
* **10 Points:** Absence or disruptive behavior during flag ceremonies[cite: 1], loitering outside school grounds[cite: 1], wandering during class hours without permission[cite: 1], forging parent/teacher signatures[cite: 1], disrespectful speech[cite: 1], unauthorized phone usage[cite: 1], skipping mandatory congregational prayers[cite: 1], damaging school facilities/property[cite: 1], tattoos or body piercings[cite: 1], unexcused absence from extracurriculars or school events[cite: 1], truancy[cite: 1], leaving school grounds without permission[cite: 1], defacing walls/desks/books[cite: 1].

### B. Severe Violations (25 – 50 Points)[cite: 1]
* **25 Points:** Bringing/smoking/vaping inside or outside school[cite: 1], possessing/distributing explicit media[cite: 1], damaging others' property[cite: 1], defaming the school or individuals online/offline[cite: 1], joining destructive/anarchic demonstrations[cite: 1], joining unauthorized external organizations[cite: 1], verbal/non-verbal bullying[cite: 1], distributing/wearing apparel with negative connotations toward the school[cite: 1].
* **50 Points:** Major destruction of school facilities and infrastructure[cite: 1].

### C. Critical / Fatal Violations (100 Points - Immediate Stage IV)[cite: 1]
* Initiating or engaging in fights, brawls, or gang assaults[cite: 1].
* Criminal/anarchic acts (extortion, theft, gambling, fraud)[cite: 1].
* Intercepting, damaging, or hijacking public transportation[cite: 1].
* Possession, distribution, or consumption of alcohol/intoxicants[cite: 1].
* Physical assault against peers, teachers, or school staff[cite: 1].
* Immoral acts, free sex, sexual harassment, distributing pornographic content[cite: 1].
* Possession, usage, or distribution of illegal narcotics (NAPZA)[cite: 1].
* Possession or usage of sharp weapons, firearms, or explosives[cite: 1].
* Involvement in criminal offenses (cyber harassment, extortion, doxxing, etc.)[cite: 1].
* Pregnancy or impregnating someone[cite: 1].
